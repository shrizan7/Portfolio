Accuracy of the study

Sets how many observations each process needs before its time counts as final. 95% and ±5% is the usual standard. Long processes often use ±10%.

Processes and observed times

One row per process. Qty says how many times the process is done on one unit of each model: 0 = not used, 1 = once, 2 = twice. Every is only for occasional work, such as a setup done once every 4 units. Each Obs column is one unit you watched: put its serial number and model at the top, then type how long the process took, in minutes. If it was done twice on that unit, type both times separated by a semicolon, like 33; 41. A * after a time leaves it out of the averages. To change the order, drag a row by its ⠿ handle (or use ↑ ↓). Press ＋ on a row to add a new process right below it.

Paste many rows from Excel

Copy the cells in Excel (with or without a header row) and paste them below. Without headers, the columns are read as Station · Process · Obs 1 · Obs 2 · … With headers, these are recognised: Station, Process (or Step, Operation, Task), Type, Crew, Every, Comes after, Own rating %, Rating reason, Own allowance %, and Qty followed by a model name (for example Qty Model C). Any other column is an observation. Put the model in square brackets after the serial number, like 105 [Model C]. Times are in the unit set on Setup. Two times in one cell (33; 41) mean the process was done twice on that unit.

Labor and time per unit, by model

Each model’s time adds up the standard time of every process it uses, times how often it uses it. The weighted average uses your real mix, and is the number to use for staffing and capacity.

Standard time per process

Tap an observation to change it, leave it out, or delete it. Orange times are more than 30% away from that process’s middle value. Standard = Average × Rating % × (1 + Allowance %), for doing the process once. If a process happens more or less than once per unit, the time per unit is shown under it. Preliminary means it doesn’t have enough observations yet. All times are in minutes.

Tap an observation to change it, leave it out, or delete it. A small number after a time marks the 2nd or 3rd time on that unit. Orange times are more than 30% away from that process’s middle value. Standard is per occurrence (one panel, one weld). Per unit is the weighted average across the mix. Preliminary means it doesn’t have enough observations yet. All times are in minutes.

How the numbers are worked out
Average (observed) = mean of every kept observation, all models and occurrences together
Normal time = Average × Rating ÷ 100
Standard time (per occurrence) = Normal time × (1 + Allowance ÷ 100)
Time per unit for a model = Standard × Qty for that model ÷ Every
Labor per unit for a model = Σ (time per unit × Crew)
Mix = model demand ÷ total demand. Weighted per unit = Σ (Mix × time per unit for that model)
Takt time = Available time per period ÷ Total units needed (all models)
Observations needed = ( z ÷ accuracy × √(n·Σx² − (Σx)²) ÷ Σx )², z = 1.645 / 1.96 / 2.576 for 90 / 95 / 99%
Same work? = flagged when the averages of two models, or of the 1st and 2nd time on a unit, differ by more than 15% and by more than twice the normal spread of that process
Minimum operators = Weighted labor per unit ÷ Takt time, rounded up
Westinghouse rating = 100 × (1 + skill + effort + conditions + consistency)

Send times to Layout Extruder

Puts your standard times on the stops of a Layout Extruder flow path, so its cycle and waiting times use real numbers. Station times are the weighted average across the mix.

  1. In Layout Extruder, press Save paths & text to file.
  2. Open that file here. Each stop gets matched to a station or process with the same name.
  3. Check the matches, save the new file, and load it back into Layout Extruder.
