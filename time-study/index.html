Time Study

1 · Settings

Rating: 100 = normal pace. Over 100 if the operator worked faster than normal, under 100 if slower. If it isn’t 100, write why in Notes.
Allowance: extra time for personal needs, fatigue and small delays. The textbook figure is 15% (personal 5 + fatigue 4 + delay 6). Use your plant’s number if it has one.

2 · Processes and times

One row per process. Each Obs column is one time you watched it, in minutes (or type 1:25:30 for hours:min:sec). Press Enter to go down a column. Drag ⠿ to change the order, ＋ adds a row below. Grey columns are worked out for you.

Paste many rows from Excel

Copy the cells in Excel and paste them below. Columns: Phase · Process · time · time · … A header row is fine (it is recognised). A file downloaded from this page can be pasted back in the same way.

3 · Totals

How the numbers are worked out
Average = mean of the times you entered
Normal = Average × Rating ÷ 100
Standard = Normal × (1 + Allowance ÷ 100)
Enough? = times needed = (1.96 ÷ accuracy × √(n·Σx² − (Σx)²) ÷ Σx)², at 95% confidence. “Yes” when you have at least that many.
Phase total = sum of the standard times in that phase. Total per unit = sum of all of them.
